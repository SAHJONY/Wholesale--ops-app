# SAHJONY Investigate
No fix before reproduction and root-cause evidence. Define the observable failure, identify last known good revision, trace request/data flow, rank hypotheses, test one variable at a time, and stop after three failed fix attempts to re-frame. Verify fixes with regression and production-relevant smoke tests. Never mutate provider credentials merely to test a hypothesis.
