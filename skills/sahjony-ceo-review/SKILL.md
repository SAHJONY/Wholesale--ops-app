# SAHJONY CEO Review
Score revenue leverage, operator time saved, deal-quality improvement, defensibility, reliability, compliance exposure, and implementation cost. Challenge whether the change improves qualified opportunities, speed-to-contact, underwriting quality, buyer matching, closing rate, or assignment revenue. Decide EXPAND, HOLD, REDUCE, or REJECT. Require measurable success metrics and a kill criterion.
