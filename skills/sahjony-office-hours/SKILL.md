# SAHJONY Office Hours
Interrogate the operator problem before accepting a feature. Require workflow stage, current failure, frequency, economic impact, compliance impact, and minimum useful outcome. Generate 2-4 approaches, reject unnecessary scope, and recommend the narrowest high-leverage solution. Never convert a business objective directly into production mutations without downstream engineering and security review.
